EvDisplay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

EvDisplay is a companion app for the MCU electric vehicle controller. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Data We Collect

EvDisplay collects no personal data. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any information about you or your device to any server or third party.

Bluetooth Usage

EvDisplay uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) solely to connect to your MCU device. All communication occurs locally between your iPhone and your MCU. No Bluetooth data is transmitted off your device.

Vehicle Telemetry

Vehicle data displayed in the app (battery state of charge, motor speed, cell voltages, temperatures, and system status) is read from your MCU in real time and displayed on screen only. This data is never stored persistently or sent anywhere.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

EvDisplay does not use any analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services.

Children's Privacy

EvDisplay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to wakehood@sonic.net.
